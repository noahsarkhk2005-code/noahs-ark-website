Perish Of Empyrean（Poe）Perish Of Empyrean (Poe)

Perish of Empyrean（Poe）係香港交響黑死金屬樂隊，2019 年成立。官方定位為 symphonic black／death metal。已發表〈Call From the Abandoned〉、〈Inborn Decadence〉、〈March of Plaint〉等單曲，並推出限量卡帶。2023 年曾於香港為 Mayhem 擔任開場。 Perish of Empyrean (Poe) is a Hong Kong symphonic black/death metal band formed in 2019. Releases include the singles “Call From the Abandoned,” “Inborn Decadence,” and “March of Plaint,” plus a limited cassette. In 2023 they opened for Mayhem in Hong Kong.
