Noah's Ark II

Hong Kong Metal Fest

Chamame Livehouse · 18:00

承接活動品牌 2005 年 Noah's Ark Hong Kong's Extreme，二十年後五支本地金屬樂隊再聚，獻上 Noah's Ark II 香港金屬音樂節。以上為活動品牌沿革，並非公司年資；主辦公司挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司正籌備成立為非牟利擔保有限公司。

Twenty years after the Hong Kong's Extreme 2005 event brand, five local metal bands returned for Noah's Ark II Hong Kong Metal Fest. That timeline is the event brand, not company seniority; the presenter Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited is being established as a company limited by guarantee.

LINE-UP

  • 招魂 Evocation
  • PROTOSS 神族
  • Perish of Empyrean
  • Chimeras
  • Cursed Eyes
