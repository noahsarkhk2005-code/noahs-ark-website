Live in Hong Kong

2005 年，活動品牌 Noah's Ark 以 Hong Kong's Extreme 之名舉辦本地極限金屬現場，是挪亞方舟系列活動的起點。

In 2005 the event brand Noah's Ark presented Hong Kong's Extreme — a live extreme-metal night that started the series.

二十年後，這個名字以 Noah's Ark II 香港金屬音樂節回歸。

Twenty years later the name returned as Noah's Ark II Hong Kong Metal Fest.
