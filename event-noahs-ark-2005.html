Hong Kong's Extreme 2005

Live in Hong Kong

2005 · Band Evocation
01 / 07

2005 年，活動品牌 Noah's Ark 以 Hong Kong's Extreme 之名舉辦本地極限金屬現場，是挪亞方舟系列活動的起點。「2005」是活動品牌名稱，並非本公司註冊或成立年份。主辦公司挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司正籌備成立為非牟利擔保有限公司，與活動品牌分開。

In 2005 the event brand Noah's Ark presented Hong Kong's Extreme — a live extreme-metal night that started the series. “2005” is an event brand name, not the company's year of incorporation. The presenter, Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited, is being established as a non-profit company limited by guarantee, separate from the event brand.

二十年後，這個名字以 Noah's Ark II 香港金屬音樂節回歸。

Twenty years later the name returned as Noah's Ark II Hong Kong Metal Fest.
