MISSION

公司宗旨 Our Mission

挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司（Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited）正籌備成立為非牟利擔保有限公司，不向成員分紅。法定名稱勿寫成「挪亞方舟有限公司」或 Noah's Ark Limited。公司與活動品牌「2005」分開：2005 是現場活動名稱，不是本公司註冊或成立年份。宗旨是現場音樂主辦、亞洲交流、公開教育。 Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited (挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司) is being established as a non-profit company limited by guarantee and does not distribute profits to members. Do not use “Noah's Ark Limited” or 挪亞方舟有限公司. The company is separate from the “2005” event brand: 2005 is a live-event name, not the year of incorporation. Our mission is live music presenting, Asia exchange, and public education.

  • 01 現場音樂主辦 Live music presenting
    主辦香港現場演出，為樂手同觀眾提供專業舞台。下一場：2026 年 12 月 5 日 Noah's Ark 2.5／PROTOSS 20th Anniversary @ MOM Livehouse。 Present Hong Kong live shows. Next: 5 Dec 2026 Noah's Ark 2.5 / PROTOSS 20th Anniversary at MOM Livehouse.
  • 02 亞洲交流 Asia exchange
    促進本地與亞洲地區音樂人、觀眾同業界交流。 Foster exchange among musicians, audiences and industry partners across Asia.
  • 03 公開教育 Public education
    籌備八節公開課（公開報名，唔限會員），暫定 2027 年 6 月或之後。課堂大綱待公布。 Preparing eight public classes with open enrolment (not members-only), planned for June 2027 or later. Syllabus to be published.

我們相信，多元現場音樂與表演藝術是城市文化活力的重要組成。挪亞方舟以專業、開放與持續的態度，推動香港表演藝術生態的健康發展。 We believe diverse live music and performing arts are vital to a city's cultural energy. Noah's Ark works with professionalism, openness and consistency to support a healthy performing arts ecosystem in Hong Kong.
