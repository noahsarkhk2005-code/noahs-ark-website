MISSION

公司宗旨 Our Mission

挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司（Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited）以推廣香港樂隊音樂文化、國際文化交流與社區教育為宗旨。非牟利擔保有限公司（籌備中），不向成員分紅。 Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited exists to promote Hong Kong band music culture, international cultural exchange, and community education. It is a non-profit company limited by guarantee (in formation) and does not distribute profits to members.

法定名稱勿寫成「挪亞方舟有限公司」或 Noah's Ark Limited。公司與活動品牌「2005」分開：2005 是現場活動名稱，不是本公司註冊或成立年份。 Do not use “Noah's Ark Limited” or 挪亞方舟有限公司. The company is separate from the “2005” event brand.

  • 01 推廣香港樂隊音樂文化 Promote Hong Kong band music culture
    透過現場演出、媒體同社群，推廣本地樂隊同香港音樂文化。 Advance local bands and Hong Kong music culture through live shows, media and community.
  • 02 國際文化交流 International cultural exchange
    促進本地與海外音樂人、觀眾同業界交流，建立跨地域音樂網絡。 Foster exchange among local and overseas musicians, audiences and industry partners.
  • 03 社區教育 Community education
    以公開、可參與嘅方式，把音樂文化帶入社區，培育觀眾同下一代音樂人。 Bring music culture into the community through open, participatory learning that grows audiences and the next generation of musicians.

我們相信，多元現場音樂與表演藝術是城市文化活力的重要組成。挪亞方舟以專業、開放與持續的態度，推動香港表演藝術生態的健康發展。 We believe diverse live music and performing arts are vital to a city's cultural energy. Noah's Ark works with professionalism, openness and consistency to support a healthy performing arts ecosystem in Hong Kong.
