MISSION

公司宗旨 Our Mission

Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited（挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司）是非牟利有限公司，專注活動主辦、社交平台、媒體內容、娛樂發展與文化教育。 Noah's Ark Culture Communication Company Limited (挪亞方舟文化傳播有限公司) is a non-profit limited company focused on event production, social platforms, media content, entertainment development and cultural education.

  • 01 主辦多元表演藝術活動 Present diverse performing arts
    策劃本地與海外樂隊現場演出、音樂節及相關表演，為音樂人提供專業舞台。 Produce live shows and festivals with local and international bands, giving artists a professional stage.
  • 02 營運社交平台與媒體內容 Run platforms & media content
    透過社交媒體及自有渠道，製作、發佈與推廣音樂及表演藝術內容。 Create and promote music and performing arts content through social and owned media channels.
  • 03 推動娛樂發展與產業連結 Connect the entertainment industry
    促進音樂人、觀眾與產業交流合作，支持娛樂多元發展。 Foster exchange between artists, audiences and industry partners to support a diverse entertainment scene.
  • 04 文化教育與觀眾拓展 Cultural education & audience growth
    舉辦工作坊、講座與推廣活動，提升公眾認識與參與，培育新一代觀眾與音樂人才。 Host workshops, talks and outreach to deepen public appreciation and nurture the next generation of audiences and musicians.

我們相信，多元現場音樂與表演藝術是城市文化活力的重要組成。挪亞方舟以專業、開放與持續的態度，推動香港表演藝術生態的健康發展。 We believe diverse live music and performing arts are vital to a city's cultural energy. Noah's Ark works with professionalism, openness and consistency to support a healthy performing arts ecosystem in Hong Kong.
